Before You Go Down

Created by: Adrian Blackshire and Claude

Far below an old ceremonial road lies a labyrinth grown from living roots, carved with druidic runes and guarded long ago by a warden named Vaelric. Travelers say nothing that goes down there comes back up. You are a lone adventurer with a pack, a belt knife and a flint, and you are going down anyway.

  • Read each scene, then pick what you do next.
  • When a check comes up, click to roll the d20. Your bonus is added, and you need to meet or beat the number shown.
  • Your character sheet tracks your HP, armor class, gold and everything you find. Your progress is saved in this browser, and you can keep up to three runs in separate save slots. Use Saves in your items to switch.
  • Your journal keeps a record of each journey in this browser: time played, deaths, gold, items, bounties and treasure. Open it from your items, and you'll get a summary at the end of every chapter.
  • In a fight, your foe's health shows on the sheet. Land hits in a row to build a combo: +1 to hit for each, +2 damage on the second, and the third is a Rootbreaker that doubles your damage dice. A miss breaks the chain.
  • Sound is off to start. Turn it on here or from your items for a quiet bed of sound in each region, and the Hesk songs sung out loud.
  • Once a fight, when a blow leaves you at 23% of your HP or less, you can dig in: stop the sweeping marker on the root to heal and harden your guard. You have 3 footholds to dig in with, and one comes back each new chapter. On the road you'll meet creatures of the realm, each with its own trick. Your journal keeps a bestiary of every one you've met, and filling it earns marks that help you on the road in every journey after.
