ENTER THE LABYRINTH

Created by: Adrian Blackshire and Claude

Descend Into The Dark

Far beneath the earth, living roots twist through forgotten halls, sealed crypts, and ruined shrines. Ancient stone corridors pulse with unnatural growth. Every path stretches deeper than it should.

Travellers have vanished here for centuries. Explorers, thieves, pilgrims, scholars. None returned unchanged.

Tonight, you follow them.

The Labyrinth Is Alive

It Watches

Every corridor shifts. Every shadow lingers too long. Something notices each step you take.

It Remembers

The roots carry echoes of everyone who entered before you. Some whispers are warnings. Others are traps.

It Hungers

The deeper you descend, the harder it becomes to remember why you came.

Before You Go Down

Created by: Adrian Blackshire and Claude

Far below an old ceremonial road lies a labyrinth grown from living roots, carved with druidic runes and guarded long ago by a warden named Vaelric. Travelers say nothing that goes down there comes back up. You are a lone adventurer with a pack, a belt knife and a flint, and you are going down anyway.

  • Read each scene, then pick what you do next.
  • When a check comes up, click to roll the d20. Your bonus is added, and you need to meet or beat the number shown.
  • Your character sheet tracks your HP, armor class, gold and everything you find. Your progress is saved in this browser.
